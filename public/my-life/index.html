Fate swipes first

An accidental swipe on a matrimony app. A profile that refused to be deleted. A quiet moment that set everything into motion.

It didn't begin the way most stories begin. There was no dramatic meeting, no grand gesture, no perfectly planned moment. It started with an accidental swipe on a matrimony app.

You accidentally swiped right on many profiles, Gopu. When you realized what happened, you rushed to delete all those interests — but fate had other plans. Out of all of them, my profile somehow remained. And that one small, accidental swipe that wasn't undone set everything into motion.

Word of the interest made its way from Mr. Gopakumar to Mr. Ashok (Athuvi). Athuvi came home and told you guys about the alliance. You checked the profile and started to like me, and you began dreaming.

chapter three · January 25, 2025

The Pennukanal

A wedding in Alappuzha, a rest stop at Club 7, a family that almost gave up waiting, and a conversation no one could end.

JAN 25The formal pennukanal was set. That day, we had been travelling — a wedding in Alappuzha first, then making our way back through Thiruvalla. Tired from the road, we stopped to rest at Club 7 in Thiruvalla — the very same place that would later become a little landmark in our story.

By the time we finally arrived at your home, it was around 3 or 4 in the afternoon. Your family had been watching the clock, anxiously wondering if we would even come.

When we started talking, something clicked almost immediately. Gayathri, Suraj — relatives and all — joined the conversation at some point, but the heart of the meeting was between us. We talked for so long that our families eventually had to call out from the other room — "how much longer will this go on?" 😄

That day I came to know that you were not the silent girl that I thought you were. The fact that it had to be interrupted said everything about how comfortable we already were with each other.

At the end, you looked at me and asked simply, "Are you okay with me?" I said I had to think about it and discuss with my family. For a moment, your heart dropped — you thought I didn't like you. But that was just me being careful and honest, the way I always am.

chapter six · early February 2025

The turning point

A fight. A cut call. A sister who picked up the phone. And the moment everything shifted.

Then came the day everything changed.

You, Gopu — exhausted from the one-sided effort and the constant I'm busy — reached your limit. In one phone call, you spoke sharply, emotionally — and cut the call.

Your sister, witnessing it all, called me herself. I picked up. And in the background, I could hear you crying.

That was the moment. Something in me shifted. I called you back. I became more present, more involved, more there — not because I was obligated to be, but because somewhere along the way, without quite noticing it, I had started to care.

We both laugh about it now. It took a fight and some tears to crack me open. But it worked.
